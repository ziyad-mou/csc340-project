## Description
> PokeVerse is an online marketplace that connects Pokémon card collectors with sellers. The app helps collectors discover Pokémon cards that match their interests and allows sellers to offer cards through their own listings. Customers can browse available cards, subscribe to services, and leave reviews, while providers can manage their profiles, create card listings, view customer statistics, and respond to reviews.

## App Functions

1. Customer (the user with the customer role):
    1. Create/modify customer profile - Customers can create a collector profile and update their personal information and collecting preferences.
    2. View available services - Customers can browse available Pokémon card listings from different sellers.
    3. Subscribe to available services - Customers can subscribe to available seller services or card listings that interest them.
    4. Write reviews for subscribed services - Customers can write reviews about services they have subscribed to.

2. Provider (the user with the provider role):
    1. Create/modify/remove provider profile - Providers can create, update, and remove their profile information.
    2. Create services - Providers can create and list services with details such as name, description, price, and availability.
    3. View customer statistics - Providers can view statistics about customers who use or subscribe to their services.
    4. Reply to reviews - Providers can respond to reviews left by customers for their services.
