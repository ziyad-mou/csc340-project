Pokémon Card Collecting 101

Learn the basics of collecting, searching, buying, and caring for Pokémon cards.

Understanding Pokémon Cards

Pokémon cards come from different sets and feature different Pokémon, artwork, attacks, abilities, and rarity levels.

2

Learn Card Rarity

Some cards are more common while others are harder to find. Rare cards may be more valuable and more desirable to collectors.

3

Check Card Condition

Card condition matters when buying and collecting. Look for descriptions such as Near Mint, Lightly Played, or Moderately Played.
